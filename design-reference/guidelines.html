The enterprise face of Aidi AI

Aidi AI is an enterprise AI platform. This is the system that dresses it: same logo, same typefaces, same navy as the Aidi Design System. What changes is emphasis — the accent recedes, structure carries the weight, and the interface reads as infrastructure rather than an app.

Built for
Business users · Analysts · IT admins · AI operators
Relationship
Cousin of the Aidi Design System, not a replacement
01 — Position

One brand, two registers

The existing system speaks to Hassan — the café owner who wants the software to feel light. Aidi AI speaks to the analyst who has it open for seven hours, the executive reading a narrative summary without an analyst in the loop, and the reviewer who has to sign off on what the model did. Those readers are not persuaded by warmth. They are persuaded by density they can trust, labels that do not move, and an answer that shows its work.

Nothing about the brand is discarded. The logo, Anodina, Manifa V2, navy, and the pale-blue surface all carry over untouched. The mesh gradient survives too, but it is quarantined to marketing, sign-in and empty states — it never sits behind data.

What Aidi AI is
An enterprise AI platform: organisations connect their business data, documents and systems, and their people interact with all of it in natural language through specialised AI agents that share context and leave an auditable trail.
The long-term goal is a central intelligence layer — enterprise knowledge, business systems, AI agents and workflow automation in one governed platform.
What it is today
A governed multi-tenant AI layer with one complete vertical application built on it: conversational business intelligence.
The governed model layer — named endpoints, weighted traffic splits, transport-only fallback, guardrails that fail closed, per-organisation egress control and per-call metering — is the platform's strongest technical asset.
The BI vertical — natural language to validated SQL to charts, dashboards, alerts and scheduled reports — is complete and works.
The acting half of the vision — tools, workflows, writes to business systems, human approval — does not exist yet.

02 — Principles

Five shifts

01
Structure over decoration
Hierarchy comes from rules, alignment and spacing. A 1px border is the default way to separate things; shadow is reserved for surfaces that genuinely float above the page.
02
Orange is a rail, not a surface
Two to four small orange touchpoints per screen — never zero, never a fill. It marks where you are (active nav rail, active tab underline), what matters (the lead KPI, the emphasised chart series) and what needs a human (attention dots). Filling every primary button with it makes the exception invisible; using none of it makes the screen someone else’s product.
03
The data is the interface
Chrome stays quiet so tables and charts read first. Tabular numerals everywhere a number appears, numbers right-aligned, units in the column header rather than repeated per cell.
04
Legible under audit
WCAG AA is the floor, not the target. Status is never carried by colour alone — it always pairs with a label, an icon or a shape. Every focus state is visible on both themes.
05
Composure in motion
Transitions confirm a change rather than perform one. Nothing bounces, scales or slides more than a few pixels. Skeletons hold layout so the page never reflows under the cursor.

04 — Typography

Same faces, different discipline

Anodina and Manifa V2 carry over. Anodina ships three weights only — Light 300, Regular 400, ExtraBold 800 — and the enterprise rules change which of them does the work.

Light 300
Retired from product UI. Allowed for marketing lead paragraphs at 20px and above, where its airiness still reads.
Regular 400
The product default. All body copy, table cells, field values, helper text. Light at 13px in a dense table is a legibility failure — this is the fix.
ExtraBold 800
Headings, KPI values, column headers, button labels, eyebrows. There is no medium, so 800 also carries emphasis inside body text.

Product scale

The marketing scale keeps its 72 / 56 / 44px display sizes. Product ratchets down and bases on 14px so that a dense screen holds more without shrinking below comfortable reading.

07 — Bilingual & RTL

Arabic is a direction, not a translation layer

The brand is Arabic-first — roughly 70 / 30 in practice — so RTL is not a variant that ships late. Every specimen in this document has an Arabic counterpart at the same density, the same control heights and the same row count. A screen that loses a column, wraps a label or drops a filter when it flips has not been localised.

Arabic type rules

Never letter-space Arabic
Tracking severs the cursive joins and turns a word into loose glyphs. Anodina's −1.5% display tracking must be zeroed for Manifa, never inherited.
الحماية
0
الحماية
.12em — joins break
Eyebrows lose the caps
Arabic is unicased, so text-transform: uppercase does nothing and the tracking that made caps readable only damages the word. The Arabic eyebrow carries the same job with Bold 700 at 12px in subtle grey.
Gross margin
الهامش الإجمالي
Step up, loosen up
Manifa's Bold is optically lighter than Anodina's ExtraBold and its ascenders and descenders reach further. Every Arabic size below 14px steps up 1px, and line-height gains roughly 0.08 so diacritics never touch the row above.
Two weights, not three
Manifa V2 ships Regular 400 and Bold 700 only. Bold covers everything Anodina used 800 for; there is no Light equivalent, which is another reason Light 300 is retired from product.

Implementation

Logical properties only
No left / right in component CSS. Direction is set once on <html dir> and everything below reads from the writing mode.
padding-inline-start
margin-inline-end
border-inline-start
inset-inline-start
text-align: start / end
Font by direction, not by string
The design system already binds Manifa to [dir="rtl"] in base.css. Enterprise adds the type corrections so Arabic screens do not inherit Latin tracking.
[dir="rtl"]{
  font-family: var(--font-arabic);
  letter-spacing: 0;
  --fw-strong: 700;
}
Sign-off rule: a screen is not complete until it has been reviewed at dir="rtl" with real Arabic strings. Latin placeholders in an RTL frame hide every wrapping and alignment fault the flip introduces.
08 — Practice

Do & don't

Do
Spend orange once per screen, on the thing the screen exists to do.
Separate with borders; save shadow for surfaces that float.
Put the mesh gradient on sign-in, marketing and empty states.
Right-align every number and set it tabular.
Pair every status colour with a label and a distinct shape.
Keep Arabic and English screens at identical density.
Wrap Latin identifiers inside Arabic copy in dir="ltr".
Don't
Fill primary buttons with orange, or ring focus in it.
Float cards on 20px radii and soft shadows in a dense view.
Run a gradient behind a table, chart or form.
Set body copy in Light 300 below 20px.
Use a third background colour — two is the ceiling per screen.
Set a headline in ALL-CAPS inside the product.
Let Arabic inherit Latin tracking, or letter-space it at all.
